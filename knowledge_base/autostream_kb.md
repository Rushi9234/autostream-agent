# AutoStream Knowledge Base

AutoStream is a SaaS product that provides automated video editing tools for content
creators on platforms like YouTube, Instagram, TikTok, and more.

## Pricing Plans

AutoStream offers two subscription plans.

### Basic Plan

- Price: $29 per month
- Videos: up to 10 videos per month
- Maximum resolution: 720p
- Suitable for hobbyists and casual creators who publish occasionally.

### Pro Plan

- Price: $79 per month
- Videos: unlimited videos per month
- Maximum resolution: 4K
- AI-powered automatic captions included
- Suitable for full-time content creators, agencies, and high-volume channels.

## Features

### Basic Plan Features

- Automated cuts and trimming
- Background music library
- Export up to 720p resolution
- Standard editing templates

### Pro Plan Features

- Everything in the Basic plan
- Unlimited video exports per month
- 4K export resolution
- AI-generated captions in multiple languages
- Priority rendering queue
- Advanced editing templates

## Company Policies

### Refund Policy

AutoStream offers refunds within 7 days of the original purchase date.
No refunds are provided after 7 days have passed since purchase.

### Support Policy

- Basic Plan users receive standard business-hours email support.
- Pro Plan users receive 24/7 support across email and chat.
- 24/7 support is available only on the Pro plan.
